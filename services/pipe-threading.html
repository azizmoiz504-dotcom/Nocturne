Pipe threading service in Al Quoz, Dubai

We thread pipe at our trade counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, so the pipe you buy from us can be threaded before you collect it. Tell us the thread type you need and we will confirm it before you come.

Tell us the thread type first

Pipe threads are not all the same. NPT and BSPT look alike but do not seal reliably together, so the thread on your pipe must match the fittings and valves it screws into.

Check the thread on the fittings you already have, or on the equipment you are connecting to, and tell us which thread you need when you send your list. Our NPT vs BSPT guide shows how to tell them apart.

What to send on WhatsApp

  • Pipe material.
  • Pipe size (nominal bore) and schedule, if you know it.
  • The thread type you need.
  • Thread one end or both ends.
  • Number of pieces to thread.
