Hydraulic hose crimping in Al Quoz, Dubai

We cut hose to length and crimp hydraulic hose at our counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3. Bring the old hose, or send the details on WhatsApp first.

What we do

Hydraulic hose crimping: we cut hydraulic hose to the length you need and crimp the fittings on. We also cut other hose to length at the counter.

Hydraulic, air, suction and diesel hose are stocked at Al Quoz, so you can buy the hose and have it made up in one visit.

Bring the old hose if you can

The old hose is the quickest way to match a replacement. It shows us the size, the length and the fittings on each end. If you cannot bring it, send the details below.

What to send on WhatsApp

  • Hose size (inside diameter).
  • The markings printed along the old hose, or a clear photo of them.
  • Length from end to end.
  • The fitting on each end: type, thread and angle (straight, 45° or 90°).
  • Quantity.

Other hose and couplings

We also list composite hose with camlock or flanged ends, S.S braided hose, green PVC suction hose, camlocks, Chicago couplings and hose clamps.
