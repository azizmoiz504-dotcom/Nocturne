How to choose brass fittings and valves

We are a brass fittings supplier in Dubai, selling threaded brass items from our Al Quoz counter. Valves: an air release valve at 1", air vents from ½" to 1", float valves with PVC ball at 2" and with copper ball at 4", and solenoid valves at ¾" and 1".

Fittings: hex nipples at ⅜" and ½", reducing bushes from ½" × ¼" to 1" × ¾", sockets at ¼" and ¾", tees at ⅜" and ½", tank connectors from ½" to 4", and test points at ¼" and ½". We also list brass round bar, 50 mm × 1 ft. Copper and brass tubes and fittings are stocked too.

A float valve shuts the inlet when a tank fills. An air release valve or air vent lets trapped air out of high points in water lines. A tank connector gives a sealed threaded outlet through a tank wall, and a test point lets you take a pressure or temperature reading.
