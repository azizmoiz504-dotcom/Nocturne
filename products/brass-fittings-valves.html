How to choose SS and brass fittings

Our SS and brass fittings are threaded and listed from ⅛" to 2": hex nipples, king nipples, female king nipples, male/female nipples, sockets, elbows, tees, end plugs, end caps and hose menders. Reducer hex nipples and reducing bushes run from ¼" × ⅛" to 2" × 1½".

We also list male tees and male elbows at ½", tank connectors from ½" to 4", brass solenoid valves at ¾" and 1", and brass test points at ¼" and ½". Copper and brass tubes and fittings are stocked too.

Brass suits water and general service; choose SS where the fluid or the site is corrosive. A tank connector gives a sealed threaded outlet through a tank wall, and a test point lets you take a pressure or temperature reading.
