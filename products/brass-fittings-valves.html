How to choose brass fittings

Our brass fittings are threaded and listed from ⅛" to 2": 45° and 90° elbows, tees, cross tees, unions, sockets, hex nipples, king nipples, female king nipples, hose menders, end plugs and end caps. Reducing bushes, reducing sockets, reducing nipples, M/F reducing nipples and reducing tees run from ¼" × ⅛" to 2" × 1½".

We also list male tees, male elbows and street elbows from ⅛" to ½", tank connectors from ½" to 4", brass solenoid valves from ¼" to 2", and brass test points at ¼", ⅜" and ½". Copper and brass tubes and fittings are stocked too.

Brass suits water and general service; where the fluid or the site is corrosive, choose our S.S threaded fittings instead. A tank connector gives a sealed threaded outlet through a tank wall, and a test point lets you take a pressure or temperature reading.
