How to choose a gate valve

Flanged (F/T) options are C.S gate valves with WCB body in CL150 and CL300 from 1" to 12", S.S gate valves in SS304 or SS316 in PN16 and CL150 from ½" to 12", C.I gate valves in PN10 to PN16 and D.I gate valves in PN16 to PN25, both from 1" to 16", and knife edge gate valves with a CI, DI, CS or SS body and an SS304 or SS316 blade, wafer, lug or flanged, in PN10, PN16 or CL150, from 2" to 12".

For small-bore lines we list brass and bronze gate valves in NPT or BSPT from ½" to 4", S.S screwed gate valves in SS304 or SS316, 200# and PN10 to PN25, up to 4", and forged gate valves in SS304/SS316 (A182) or carbon steel A105 at 600 to 800 WOG (Class 800), in SW or NPT from ½" to 2".

A gate valve is for on/off isolation. Run it fully open or fully closed. It is not made for throttling, so use a globe valve where you need to regulate flow. Pick CS WCB or forged A105 for oil, gas and process lines, SS where corrosion matters, and CI or brass for water.

Questions buyers ask

Do you have CS gate valves in class 300?

Yes. Our C.S gate valve with WCB body is listed in CL150 and CL300, flanged, from 1" to 12". Send the size and quantity on WhatsApp for a quote.

Can I use a gate valve to control flow?

No. A gate valve should be fully open or fully closed. For throttling, choose a globe valve; we list them in CS, SS, CI and forged A105.

What is the difference between the forged SS and forged A105 gate valves?

Both are 600 to 800 WOG (Class 800), from ½" to 2", with SW or NPT ends. The forged SS304 or SS316 body is for corrosive or clean service; the forged A105 carbon steel body is for general oil, gas and process lines.
