How to choose a check valve

Our check valves (non-return valves) cover swing, spring, piston, wafer disk, dual-disk and grooved designs. Flanged: C.S (ASTM A216 WCB) and S.S (SS304 or SS316, ASTM A351) in CL150 and CL300 from ½" to 12", and C.I (ASTM A126, EN-GJL-250) in PN16 from 1½" to 8". Wafer disk type in PN10 and PN16 from 1½" to 8": C.I and C.S with a D.I or S.S disk, and S.S; plus S.S in PN40 (PN25 above DN100) from 1" to 8". Wafer dual-disk type: C.I in PN16 with a D.I or S.S disk, and S.S in PN16 or PN25, both from 2" to 12". For grooved lines there is a D.I grooved check valve (ASTM A536) at 300 PSI or PN16 from 2½" to 8".

Screwed, from ½" to 4": brass swing, brass vertical spring and bronze swing; S.S swing in 200#, BSP or NPT; S.S vertical spring at 600 or 1000 WOG, BSP or NPT; and a forged S.S piston check valve at 800 WOG in BSP, NPT or SW. A forged steel A105 piston check valve in Class 800 (800 WOG) comes in NPT or SW from ½" to 2".

Swing check valves suit horizontal lines with steady flow. Spring and vertical check valves close faster and can sit in vertical lines with upward flow. Wafer and dual-disk types are short and light, so they fit between flanges where space is tight, such as after a pump.

Questions buyers ask

Is a check valve the same as a non-return valve?

Yes. Both names mean a valve that lets flow pass one way and stops it coming back.

Do you have a check valve for a vertical line?

Yes. We list a brass vertical spring check valve and an S.S (SS304 or SS316) vertical spring check valve at 600 or 1000 WOG in BSP or NPT, both from ½" to 4".

Which check valve should go after a pump?

Dual-disk and spring types close quickly and are common on pump discharge. Send the line size and flange class on WhatsApp and we will suggest a match from our list.

Can’t find your size or part?

Send a photo of it, or its nameplate, on WhatsApp and we will tell you if we have it.
