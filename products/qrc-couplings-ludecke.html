How to choose a QRC coupling and clamp

We list brass QRC (quick-release) couplings, mostly used on compressed air lines. Hose bodies and hose plugs come in ¼", 5/16", ⅜" and ½" and in 6, 8, 10 and 12 mm. Male bodies, female bodies, male plugs and female plugs come in ¼", ⅜" and ½".

Hose clamps sit on the same page: single bolt clamps in CS zinc plated or SS from 17-19 mm to 292-304 mm, and double bolt clamps from SL 29 to SL 675.

The body is the socket half, which usually stays on the supply side; the plug goes on the tool or hose. Plugs and bodies must be from the same series to connect, so if you are adding to an existing set, send a photo of the plug you already use. For clamps, measure the outside of the hose over the fitting and pick the range that covers it. SS clamps suit wet or outdoor use.

For a quote, WhatsApp the part (body or plug; hose, male or female), size and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Will your QRC plug fit my existing coupling?

Only if they are the same series. Send a photo of your plug or body on WhatsApp and we will check it against our list.

How do I size a bolt clamp?

Measure the outside diameter of the hose where it sits on the fitting, then choose the clamp range that covers it. Single bolt clamps run from 17-19 mm to 292-304 mm.

Zinc plated or stainless clamp?

Zinc plated CS clamps suit dry, indoor work. Choose SS for wet, outdoor or corrosive conditions.
