How to choose a camlock type

We list camlock couplings by type. Types A, B, D and F come with BSPT or NPT threads, types C and E have a hose shank, and DC and DP are the cap and plug, all from ½" to 6" in aluminium, stainless, brass and PVC.

Doubles and reducers include type AA and DD (1½" × 1½" to 4" × 4"), DA (2" × 1½" to 4" × 3"), AR (3" × 2" to 3" × 4") and DR (2" × 1½" to 3" × 2"), types CR and ER (1" to 4" × 3"), plus an API tank truck adaptor at 6" × 4". We also list brass and aluminium camlock couplings from ½" to 6" in NPT or BSPT, PVC camlock couplings from 1" to 3", and stainless reducing camlock couplings from 2" to 4".

The letter tells you the halves. A, E and F are male adapters; B, C and D are female couplers. A and D end in a female thread, B and F in a male thread, and C and E in a hose shank. DC is the dust cap for a male adapter and DP the dust plug for a female coupler.

For a quote, WhatsApp the type letter, size, material, thread (NPT or BSPT) and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Which camlock type goes on a hose?

Type C (female coupler) and type E (male adapter) have a hose shank. Clamp the hose over the shank.

Do your camlocks come in NPT and BSPT?

Yes. Types A, B, D and F are listed in both NPT and BSPT.
