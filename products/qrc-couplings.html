How to choose a QRC coupling

We list QRC (quick-release) couplings, mostly used on compressed air lines. Hose ends and hose plugs come in ¼", 5/16", ⅜" and ½" and in 6, 8, 10 and 12 mm. Male thread couplings, female bodies, female plugs and male plugs come in ¼", ⅜" and ½".

Each part is listed in brass and in a carbon steel and stainless version.

The body is the socket half, which usually stays on the supply side; the plug goes on the equipment or hose. Plugs and bodies must be from the same series to connect, so if you are adding to an existing set, send a photo of the plug you already use. Hose clamps are on our hose clamp page.

For a quote, WhatsApp the part (body or plug; hose, male or female), size, brass or steel, and the quantity.
