How to choose a globe valve

Flanged (F/T) options are C.S globe valves with WCB body in CL150 and CL300 from ½" to 12", S.S globe valves in CF8, CL150, from ½" to 6", and C.I globe valves in PN16 from ½" to 4".

Screwed and socket-weld options are S.S low pressure 200# in NPT or BSPT up to 4", SS 316 at 600 and 800 WOG in SW or NPT, and forged steel A105 800# in SW or NPT, all from ½".

Choose a globe valve when you need to throttle or regulate flow, for example on a bypass, a drain or a cooling water line. The flow turns inside the body, so a globe valve has more pressure drop than a gate valve. For plain on/off isolation, a gate or ball valve is usually the better fit.

Questions buyers ask

Globe valve or gate valve: which do I need?

Use a globe valve to regulate flow and a gate valve for on/off isolation. If the valve will sit part-open in service, choose a globe valve.

What does CF8 mean on the SS globe valve?

CF8 is the cast stainless grade that matches 304. Our S.S globe valve in CF8 is CL150, flanged, from ½" to 6".

Do you have a forged globe valve for socket weld?

Yes. The forged steel globe valve A105 800# comes in SW or NPT from ½" to 2". There is also an SS 316 version at 600 and 800 WOG.
