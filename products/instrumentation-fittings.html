How to choose compression fittings

We supply S.S compression fittings with nut and ferrule from our Al Quoz counter. They fit 6 to 12 mm tube, with ¼" to ½" connections.

A compression fitting grips the tube with a ferrule as the nut is tightened, so it seals without welding or threading the tube. Match the fitting to the outside diameter of the tube, and use stainless fittings on stainless tube. Pressure gauges are on our pressure gauges page, and the S.S needle valve is on our ball valves page.

For a quote, WhatsApp the tube size, the connection size and thread, and the quantity. If you are matching an existing fitting, a photo of it next to a tape measure helps us check the size.
