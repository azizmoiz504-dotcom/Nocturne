How to choose a gasket

Ring gaskets come in CNAF, non-metallic, EPDM and NBR, raised face (RF) or full face (FF), for PN16, CL150, CL300 and CL600 flanges, from ½" up to any size. Spiral wound gaskets in SS304 or SS316, 2-ring or 3-ring, are listed for PN16 and CL150 to CL600, also from ½" up to any size.

Sheet: CNAF and non-metallic gasket sheet from 1 to 6 mm in 1.5 m × 1.5 m, and rubber, neoprene, silicone, Teflon or nylon sheet from 1 to 12 mm in 1.2 m × 10 m (20 kg). We also list PTFE, graphite and fiberglass gland packing from 6 mm to 50 mm (¼" to 2"), EPDM camlock washers and NBR Chicago coupling washers from ½" to 2".

A ring gasket sits inside the bolts on a raised-face flange; a full face gasket covers the whole face and suits flat-face flanges. Spiral wound gaskets are for higher pressure and temperature.

For a quote, WhatsApp the flange size, class or PN, face type, gasket type, thickness and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Ring gasket or full face gasket?

Use a ring gasket on raised-face flanges and a full face gasket on flat-face flanges, such as many cast iron valves and pumps.

Which class are your spiral wound gaskets?

They are listed for PN16, CL150, CL300 and CL600, in SS304 or SS316, from ½" up to any size. Send the flange size and class with your list.

Which gland packing do you have?

PTFE, graphite and fiberglass gland packing from 6 mm to 50 mm (¼" to 2"). Send the packing size with your list.
