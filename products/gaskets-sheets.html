How to choose a gasket

We are a gasket supplier in Dubai with a counter in Al Quoz. CNAF and rubber gaskets come ring or full face, 1.5 mm or 3 mm thick, for CL150 to CL600 and PN16 to PN40 flanges, from ½" up to any size. Spiral wound gaskets come with graphite or PTFE filler for CL150 to CL1500 from ½" to 24". RTJ ring gaskets come in stainless or soft iron.

Sheet: CNAF and metallic gasket sheet from 1 to 6 mm in 1.5 m × 1.5 m, and neoprene rubber sheet from 1 to 16 mm in 1.2 m × 10 m. We also list graphited gland packing from 6 mm to 25 mm and rubber washers for Chicago, camlock, Storz, Bauer and Miller couplings.

A ring gasket sits inside the bolts on a raised-face flange; a full face gasket covers the whole face and suits flat-face flanges. Spiral wound gaskets are for higher pressure and temperature. RTJ rings fit ring-type joint flanges in oil and gas lines.

For a quote, WhatsApp the flange size, class or PN, face type, gasket type, thickness and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Ring gasket or full face gasket?

Use a ring gasket on raised-face flanges and a full face gasket on flat-face flanges, such as many cast iron valves and pumps.

Graphite or PTFE spiral wound gasket?

Graphite filler is the general choice for heat. PTFE filler suits chemical service. Both are listed for CL150 to CL1500.

Stainless or soft iron RTJ ring?

The ring should be softer than the flange groove. Soft iron is usual on carbon steel flanges; stainless on stainless flanges.
