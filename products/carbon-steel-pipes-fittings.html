How to choose carbon steel pipe and fittings

M.S pipe, ERW (ASTM A53) and seamless (ASTM A106 GR.B), is listed in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 12". C.S butt-weld fittings (ASTM A234 WPB) come in the same schedules from ½" to 24": 45° and 90° elbows in short and long radius, 180° elbows, equal tees and end caps, plus reducing tees and concentric and eccentric reducers from ¾" × ½" to 24" × 22".

C.S swage nipples, concentric and eccentric, are made to MSS SP-95 with BSP or NPT ends from ¾" × ½" to 8" × 6". M.S long nipples run from ¼" to 8": ERW in SCH40 and SCH80, and seamless in SCH40 to SCH160.

For small-bore lines, forged C.S socket weld fittings (2000, 3000 and 6000 psi) and threaded fittings (CL1000 to CL6000) run from ½" to 4", and C.S A105 flanges go up to 24". Match the fitting schedule to the pipe wall so the bores line up for welding. We also offer pipe threading at the counter.

Questions buyers ask

What schedules do you have for MS pipe?

M.S pipe, ERW and seamless, is listed in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 12". Send the size, schedule and quantity on WhatsApp.

Is MS the same as carbon steel?

In the UAE trade, MS (mild steel) and CS (carbon steel) are often used for the same low-carbon steel pipe and fittings. Send the grade on your drawing or specification and we will confirm what we can supply.

Can you thread the MS pipe for me?

Yes, we offer pipe threading at the counter. Tell us the thread type (NPT or BSPT) when you send your list.

Do you have SCH160 butt-weld fittings?

Yes. Our C.S butt-weld elbows, tees, reducers and end caps are listed in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 up to 24".
