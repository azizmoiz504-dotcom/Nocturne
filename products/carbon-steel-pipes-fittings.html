How to choose carbon steel pipe and fittings

M.S and C.S pipe is stocked at our Al Quoz counter in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 12". Pipe is the line buyers ask us for most, so send the size, schedule and length you need.

M.S butt-weld fittings come in the same schedules from ½" to 24": 45° and 90° elbows in short and long radius, equal tees and end caps, plus reducing tees and concentric and eccentric reducers from ¾" × ½" to 24" × 22". We also list M.S swage nipples in 1000# and 3000# NPT from ¾" × ½" to 8" × 6", and A105 long nipples, SMLS or ERW, in SCH40 to SCH160.

For small-bore lines, forged C.S socket weld and threaded fittings run from ½" to 4" in CL1000 to CL6000, and MS A105 flanges come in SORF, WNRF, SWRF, blind and threaded up to 24". Match the fitting schedule to the pipe wall so the bores line up for welding. We also offer pipe threading at the counter.

Send the size, schedule, end type and quantity on WhatsApp and we will quote.

Questions buyers ask

What schedules do you have for MS pipe?

M.S and C.S pipe is listed in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 12". Send the size, schedule and quantity on WhatsApp.

Is MS the same as carbon steel?

In the UAE trade, MS (mild steel) and CS (carbon steel) are often used for the same low-carbon steel pipe and fittings. Send the grade on your drawing or specification and we will confirm what we can supply.

Can you thread the MS pipe for me?

Yes, we offer pipe threading at the counter. Tell us the thread type (NPT or BSPT) when you send your list.

Do you have SCH160 butt-weld fittings?

Yes. Our M.S butt-weld elbows, tees, reducers and end caps are listed in SCH STD, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 up to 24".

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Yes. We deliver anywhere in the UAE for orders from AED 5,000. Smaller orders can be collected from our Al Quoz counter.
