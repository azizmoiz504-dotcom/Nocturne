How to choose a pressure gauge

Our pressure gauges have a full S.S body and are liquid filled, in 4" or 2½" dial, with ranges up to 15,000 PSI and a ½" or ¼" bottom connection. You can collect them from our counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3.

Pick a gauge range so the normal working pressure sits around the middle of the dial; it is easier to read and the gauge lasts longer. Liquid filling damps needle flutter from pumps and vibration. A needle valve in front of the gauge lets you isolate it for change-out; our S.S needle valve is on the ball valves page.

For a quote, WhatsApp the dial size, range, connection size and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

What range of pressure gauge should I buy?

Choose a range where your normal working pressure sits around the middle of the dial. Our liquid-filled SS gauges go up to 15,000 PSI.

4" or 2½" dial?

A 4" dial is easier to read from a distance; a 2½" dial suits tight spots and small equipment. Both are listed.

What connection do the gauges have?

A bottom connection in ½" or ¼".
