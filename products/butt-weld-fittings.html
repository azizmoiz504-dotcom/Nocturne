How to choose butt-weld fittings

C.S and S.S butt-weld fittings are made to ASME B16.9 in SCH10, SCH20, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 24": 45° and 90° long radius elbows, 90° short radius elbows, 180° long radius return bends, equal and reducing tees, concentric and eccentric reducers, and end caps. Reducing tees and reducers run from ¾" × ½" to 24" × 20".

Long radius (LR) elbows have a centre-to-face of 1.5 times the pipe size and give a smoother flow. Short radius (SR) elbows, at 1 times, save space. Use a concentric reducer on vertical lines and an eccentric reducer on pump suction, flat side up, so air does not collect. Match the fitting schedule to the pipe wall so the bores line up for welding.

For a quote, WhatsApp the fitting, size, schedule, C.S or S.S, and the quantity.

Questions buyers ask

LR or SR elbow?

Choose LR for most lines. Use SR only where space is tight.

Concentric or eccentric reducer?

Concentric keeps the centre line and suits vertical runs. Eccentric keeps one side flat and is used on pump suction and pipe racks.

Do you have the pipe as well?

Yes. We list M.S pipe from ¼" to 24" and SS 316L/304L pipe from ⅛" to 12" on our carbon steel and stainless steel pages.
