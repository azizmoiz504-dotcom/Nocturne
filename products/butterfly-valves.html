How to choose a butterfly valve

We list C.I body butterfly valves from 1½" to 12". Lock and lever valves in PN16 come with a DI disc or an S.S disc. The lug type has an S.S disc and is listed in PN16 and CL150, also from 1½" to 12".

Butterfly valves are short, light and quick to open, so they suit water and cooling lines where a gate valve would be heavy. A lock and lever lets you set the disc and hold it in place. Choose the lug type if you need to take off the pipe on one side while the other side stays in service. An S.S disc gives more corrosion resistance than a DI disc.

Match the valve to the flanges it sits between: PN16 and CL150 drilling are not the same at most sizes, so check the mating flange before you order.

Questions buyers ask

PN16 or CL150: which butterfly valve do I need?

Match the valve to the flanges on your line. The lock and lever valves are PN16, and the lug type with S.S disc is listed in both PN16 and CL150.

What sizes do you list?

All three C.I butterfly valves on our list run from 1½" to 12". For a larger size, send the details on WhatsApp and we will tell you what we can offer.

DI disc or SS disc?

A DI disc suits general water service. Choose an S.S disc where the water or fluid is more corrosive.
