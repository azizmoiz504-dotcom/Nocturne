How to choose stainless steel pipe and fittings

SS pipe and tubing comes in 316L and 304L, SMLS and ERW, SCH10, SCH40 and SCH80, from ⅛" to 12" in 6 m lengths. B/W fittings are A403 316L, SMLS and ERW, SCH10 to SCH80, from ½" to 24".

Forged A182 316L fittings are 3000# in NPT or SW from ¼" to 4". Low pressure SS 316 fittings are CL150 in NPT or BSPT from ⅛" to 4". Long and close nipples are A182 316L, SCH10 to SCH80, from ¼" to 4" × 6".

SS 316L contains molybdenum and resists chlorides better than 304L, so it is the usual pick near the sea, in cooling water and in chemical lines. 304L suits general clean service. The L grades are low carbon, which helps when you weld.
