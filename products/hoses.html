How to choose an industrial hose

Composite hose comes as an assembly with M/F camlock ends from 1½" to 4", in lengths from 2 m to 30 m, or with PN16 or CL150 flanged ends at 2", from 1 m to 4.5 m. Oil and water suction and discharge hoses run from 1" to 4", rated 150 psi (10 bar) or 300 psi (20 bar).

PVC hoses include green suction hose from 1" to 4" and at 2½", braided and clear hose from ¼" to 1" (300 psi), steel wire hose from ½" to 4" (4 to 8 bar) and lay flat hose from 2" to 6" (2 to 4 bar).

Hydraulic, air, suction and diesel hose are also stocked at Al Quoz. We cut hose to length and crimp hydraulic hose at the counter.

Composite hose is light and flexible for transfer from tankers. Suction hose keeps its shape under vacuum, so it suits pump inlets; lay flat hose is for discharge only. Always check that the hose suits the fluid you are moving.

For a quote, WhatsApp the hose type, inside diameter, length, end fittings, the fluid and the quantity.
