How to choose a quick coupling

We list QRC (quick-release) couplings, mostly used on compressed air lines. Hose ends and hose plugs come in ¼", 5/16", ⅜" and ½" and in 6, 8, 10 and 12 mm. Male thread couplings, female bodies, female plugs and male plugs come in ¼", ⅜" and ½". Each part is listed in brass and in a carbon steel and stainless version.

We also list Storz couplings from 1½" to 4" in aluminium and stainless steel, plus galvanised Bauer and Millar (Miller) couplings from 3" to 8" for quick hose connections.

Storz couplings are symmetrical: two of the same size lock together with no male or female half, which makes them common on water and pump hoses. Bauer couplings use a ball-and-socket style joint that allows some angle, which helps on long lines laid over uneven ground. Camlock couplings are on our camlock couplings page.

The body is the socket half, which usually stays on the supply side; the plug goes on the equipment or hose. Plugs and bodies must be from the same series to connect, so if you are adding to an existing set, send a photo of the plug you already use. Hose clamps are on our hose clamp page.

Ductile iron universal pipe joining couplings and universal flange adapters, PN10 to PN16, are listed from 2" to 24". A universal coupling joins two plain pipe ends without welding or threading, a common way to repair a damaged section or connect new pipe to old. A universal flange adapter turns a plain pipe end into a flanged end. Measure the actual outside diameter of the pipe before you order, because pipes of the same nominal size can differ by material.

For a quote, WhatsApp the coupling type, size, hose end and the quantity. For a QRC part, say body or plug and brass or steel. For a universal coupling or flange adapter, add the measured outside diameter and the pipe material.

Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between a Storz and a camlock coupling?

A Storz coupling has two identical halves that twist together. A camlock has a male adapter and a female coupler held by cam arms.

Will your QRC plug fit my existing coupling?

Only if they are the same series. Send a photo of your plug or body on WhatsApp and we will check it against our list.

Brass or steel QRC?

Each part is listed in brass and in a carbon steel and stainless version. Send a photo of the part it has to connect to and we confirm which one fits.

Do I need to weld or thread the pipe for a universal coupling?

No. Universal couplings and flange adapters fit onto plain pipe ends.

Do you have rubber washers for these couplings?

Yes. Rubber washers for Chicago, camlock, Storz, Bauer and Miller couplings are listed on our gaskets page.

Which camlock type do I need?

It depends on which half you need and whether it ends in a thread or a hose shank. Our camlock couplings page lists types A to F, DC and DP.

Can’t find your size or part?

Send a photo of it, or its nameplate, on WhatsApp and we will tell you if we have it.
