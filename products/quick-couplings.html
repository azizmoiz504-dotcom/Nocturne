How to choose a quick coupling

QRC (quick-release) couplings for compressed air lines come in brass and in a C.P carbon steel and stainless version. Hose ends and hose plugs are listed in ¼", 5/16", ⅜" and ½" and in 6, 8, 10 and 12 mm; male thread couplings, female bodies, male plugs and female plugs in ¼", ⅜" and ½". Plugs and bodies must be from the same series to connect.

Storz couplings in aluminium or stainless steel (SS304 or SS316), and a second aluminium coupling type, run from 1½" to 4" as female, male, hose end, dust cap, reducer or gasket. Storz halves are identical and twist together, which makes them common on water and pump hoses. Galvanised Bauer and Millar couplings run from 3" to 8", complete or as single parts, including Bauer with flange and Millar with male thread.

Ductile iron universal pipe joining couplings and universal flange adapters, PN10 or PN16, run from 2" to 24". A universal coupling joins two plain pipe ends without welding or threading; a flange adapter turns a plain end into a flanged one. Measure the actual outside diameter of the pipe first.

Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between a Storz and a camlock coupling?

A Storz coupling has two identical halves that twist together. A camlock has a male adapter and a female coupler held by cam arms.

Will your QRC plug fit my existing coupling?

Only if they are the same series. Send a photo of your plug or body on WhatsApp and we will check it against our list.

Do I need to weld or thread the pipe for a universal coupling?

No. Universal couplings and flange adapters fit onto plain pipe ends.

Do you have gaskets for these couplings?

Yes. Storz, Bauer and Millar gaskets are listed as single parts on this page, and camlock and Chicago coupling washers are on our gaskets page.

Can’t find your size or part?
