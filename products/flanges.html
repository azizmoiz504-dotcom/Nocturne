How to choose a flange

SS flanges are ASTM A182 F304/304L and F316/316L and MS flanges are ASTM A105, each in SORF, SWRF, WNRF, blind and threaded from ½" to 24". SS flanges come in PN16, CL150 to CL600, and 5K, 10K and 16K. MS flanges come in PN10 to PN25, CL150 to CL600, and 5K, 10K and 16K.

Slip-on (SORF) flanges are easy to fit and common on low and medium pressure lines. Weld neck (WNRF) flanges are for higher pressure, temperature or cyclic load. Socket weld (SWRF) suits small bore, threaded flanges avoid welding, and blind flanges close off a line or a nozzle.

Class 150, PN16 and JIS 10K drilling do not match at most sizes, so check the standard on the mating flange; our flange chart lists Class 150 and Class 300 dimensions. For a quote, WhatsApp the size, flange type, class, PN or K, material and quantity, plus the pipe schedule for weld neck.

Questions buyers ask

CL150 or PN16 flange?

CL150 is the ASME class and PN16 the EN pressure number. Their bolt patterns differ at most sizes, so match the flange to what it bolts to. Our flange chart shows the Class 150 and Class 300 dimensions.

SORF or WNRF?

SORF slips over the pipe and suits general service. WNRF is butt-welded to the pipe and is the choice for higher pressure or temperature.

Do you have gaskets for these flanges?

Yes. CNAF, rubber, spiral wound and RTJ gaskets are listed on our gaskets page.
