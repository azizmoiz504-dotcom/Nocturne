How to choose instrumentation fittings and gauges

We supply instrumentation fittings in Dubai from our Al Quoz counter. S.S compression fittings with nut and ferrule fit 6 to 12 mm tube, with ¼" to ½" connections. S.S needle valves come in 1000#, 3000#, 6000# and 10000#, with F/F or M/F NPT ends, from ¼" to 1".

Pressure gauges have a full S.S body and are liquid filled, in 4" or 2½" dial, with ranges up to 15,000 PSI and a ½" or ¼" bottom connection.

Pick a gauge range so the normal working pressure sits around the middle of the dial; it is easier to read and the gauge lasts longer. Liquid filling damps needle flutter from pumps and vibration. A needle valve in front of the gauge lets you isolate it for change-out, and its class should match the line pressure.

For a quote, WhatsApp the tube size or thread, class, end type (F/F or M/F), dial size, range, connection size and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

What range of pressure gauge should I buy?

Choose a range where your normal working pressure sits around the middle of the dial. Our liquid-filled SS gauges go up to 15,000 PSI.

Do you have 10,000 psi needle valves?

Yes. Our S.S needle valve is listed in 1000#, 3000#, 6000# and 10000#, NPT, from ¼" to 1".

F/F or M/F needle valve?

F/F has female threads on both ends; M/F has one male and one female end, which often screws straight into a port. Choose by what you are connecting.
