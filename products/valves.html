CS or SS valves?

CS valves (WCB cast body or forged A105) are the usual choice for oil, gas and process lines. SS valves (CF8, CF8M or 316L) are for corrosive, clean or chloride service. CI, brass and bronze bodies suit water and general building services.

Classes and end connections

Flanged (F/T) valves are listed in CL150 and CL300, PN16 and PN25. Screwed and socket-weld valves are listed from 200# up to 1000 WOG, with 3000# on selected SS and MS ball valves. Ends come in NPT, BSPT, SW or flanged, depending on the valve.

Questions buyers ask

Which valve do I need for on/off isolation?

A gate valve or a ball valve. For water lines, a butterfly valve is lighter and shorter; ours run from 1½" to 12".

Which valve do I use to control flow?

A globe valve. Gate valves should only be used fully open or fully closed.
