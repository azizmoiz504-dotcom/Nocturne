How to choose a ball valve

We list SS 316 ball valves in several bodies: SS316L 1PC, 2PC and 3PC at 1000 WOG (2PC and 3PC in CF8M) with NPT or BSPT ends (3PC also SW), ¼" to 4", and SS316L 2PC flanged in PN16, CL150 and CL300 up to 12". There is also an SS 3-way at 1000 WOG and an SS 3PC at 3000# from ¼" to 2".

Other bodies: C.S WCB 2PC flanged in CL150 and CL300 up to 12", C.S 3PC flanged CL150 up to 6", C.S 3PC A105 800#, MS 1PC and 2PC at 1000 PSI (2PC also 3000 PSI), C.I 2PC PN16, brass up to 6" and bronze up to 4", plus a hydraulic high pressure ball valve 600# NPT.

A ball valve gives quick quarter-turn shut-off. A 1PC body is compact but cannot be opened for repair. A 2PC body is the general-purpose choice. A 3PC body lets you swing the centre out for service while the ends stay in the line, which helps on socket-weld lines. Use a 3-way valve to divert flow.

For a quote, WhatsApp the size, body (1PC, 2PC, 3PC or 3-way), pressure class, end connection, material and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Should I buy a 1PC, 2PC or 3PC ball valve?

Choose 1PC for a low-cost, compact valve, 2PC for general service, and 3PC where you need to service the valve in the line, especially with SW ends.

Do you have 3000# ball valves?

Yes. We list an SS 3PC ball valve at 3000# in NPT or BSPT from ¼" to 2", and an MS 2PC ball valve at 3000 PSI NPT from ½" to 2".

What is CF8M?

CF8M is the cast stainless grade that matches 316. Our SS316L 2PC and 3PC ball valves are CF8M.
