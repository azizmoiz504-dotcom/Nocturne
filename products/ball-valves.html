How to choose a ball valve

We list SS 316 ball valves in CF8M in several bodies: 1PC, 2PC and 3PC at 1000 WOG with NPT or BSPT ends (3PC also SW and 3000 WOG), ¼" to 4", and 2PC flanged in PN16, CL150 and CL300 up to 12". There is also an SS 3-way at 1000 WOG from ¼" to 4".

Other bodies: C.S WCB 2PC flanged in CL150 and CL300 up to 12", C.S 3PC flanged CL150 up to 6", MS 2PC at 1000 WOG and MS 3PC at 800# from ½" to 4", MS 1PC at 1000 PSI up to 2", C.I 2PC flanged PN16 up to 6", and brass and bronze up to 6", plus a hydraulic high pressure ball valve 600# NPT and an S.S needle valve from 1000# to 10000# NPT, ¼" to 1".

A ball valve gives quick quarter-turn shut-off. A 1PC body is compact but cannot be opened for repair. A 2PC body is the general-purpose choice. A 3PC body lets you swing the centre out for service while the ends stay in the line, which helps on socket-weld lines. Use a 3-way valve to divert flow.

For a quote, WhatsApp the size, body (1PC, 2PC, 3PC or 3-way), pressure class, end connection, material and quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Should I buy a 1PC, 2PC or 3PC ball valve?

Choose 1PC for a low-cost, compact valve, 2PC for general service, and 3PC where you need to service the valve in the line, especially with SW ends.

Do you have 3000# ball valves?

Yes. Our SS CF8M 3PC ball valve is listed at 1000 and 3000 WOG in NPT, BSPT or SW from ¼" to 4".

What is CF8M?

CF8M is the cast stainless grade that matches 316. Our SS ball valves are CF8M.

Do you have needle valves?

Yes. Our S.S needle valve is listed in 1000#, 3000#, 6000# and 10000#, F/F or M/F NPT, from ¼" to 1".
