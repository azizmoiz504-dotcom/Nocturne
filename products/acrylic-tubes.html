How to choose an acrylic tube

We list clear acrylic tube with a 2 mm wall, from 8 mm to 75 mm, and brass tank level cocks, threaded, from 12 mm × ½" to 25 mm × 1", at our counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3.

Acrylic tube is clear, light and easy to cut, which is why it is used for tank level gauges and sight glasses. It suits low pressure service at normal temperatures. Check the fluid and the temperature first, because acrylic is not meant for hot liquids or high pressure. Tank level cocks hold the tube on the tank so you can read the level.

For a quote, WhatsApp the tube size, the length you need, the cock size and the quantity. If you are replacing an old tube, send its size or a photo with a tape measure.
