How to choose an acrylic tube

We list clear acrylic tube with a 2 mm wall, from 8 mm to 75 mm, at our counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3.

Acrylic tube is clear, light and easy to cut, which is why it is used for tank level gauges and sight glasses. It suits low pressure service at normal temperatures. Check the fluid and the temperature first, because acrylic is not meant for hot liquids or high pressure. For tank level gauges, pair it with our brass tank level cocks from 12 mm × ½" to 25 mm × 1".

For a quote, WhatsApp the tube size, the length you need and the quantity. If you are replacing an old tube, send its size or a photo with a tape measure.
