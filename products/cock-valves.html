How to choose a cock valve

We list cock valves in brass and stainless steel, all threaded: a brass drain cock at ½", brass tank level cocks from 12 mm × ½" to 25 mm × 1", an S.S cock valve at ½" × 12 mm and an S.S hex type cock valve at ½".

A drain cock lets you empty a tank, pump casing or low point. Tank level cocks hold a sight tube so you can read the level in the tank; our clear acrylic tubes from 8 mm to 75 mm are made for that job. An S.S cock valve is a simple way to isolate a small connection, and stainless is the better choice where the fluid or the surroundings are corrosive.

For a quote, WhatsApp the item, size, brass or S.S, and the quantity. For a level gauge, add the tube size and length so we can match the acrylic tube.
