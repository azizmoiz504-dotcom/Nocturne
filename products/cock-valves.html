How to choose a hose clamp

We list single bolt hose clamps in zinc plated carbon steel or SS from 17-19 mm to 292-304 mm, double bolt clamps from SL 29 to SL 675, and galvanised four bolt clamps from ½" to 3". An S.S cock valve, threaded, ½" × 12 mm, is on the same page.

Measure the outside of the hose over the fitting and pick the clamp range that covers it. Zinc plated and galvanised clamps suit dry, indoor work; SS clamps suit wet, outdoor or corrosive conditions.

For a quote, WhatsApp the clamp type, size range, zinc plated, galvanised or SS, and the quantity.

Questions buyers ask

How do I size a bolt clamp?

Measure the outside diameter of the hose where it sits on the fitting, then choose the clamp range that covers it. Single bolt clamps run from 17-19 mm to 292-304 mm.

Zinc plated or stainless clamp?

Zinc plated CS clamps suit dry, indoor work. Choose SS for wet, outdoor or corrosive conditions.
