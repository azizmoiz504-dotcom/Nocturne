Oilfield supplies in Dubai: unions, valves, fittings and gaskets

We list oilfield supplies in Dubai at our counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, with delivery anywhere in the UAE for orders from AED 5,000, including Musaffah and Jebel Ali.

Hammer unions

Wing-nut hammer unions, blanking unions and hose connectors for high-pressure oilfield lines. Order by figure number and size, because different figures do not connect.

High pressure valves

  • SS ball valve 3PC, 3000#, NPT or BSPT, ¼" to 2".
  • MS ball valve 2PC, 1000 and 3000 PSI, NPT, ½" to 2".
  • Hydraulic high pressure ball valve, 600#, NPT, ¼" to 2".
  • Forged steel A105 gate, globe and check valves, 800 WOG or 800#, SW or NPT, ½" to 2".
  • C.S WCB gate, globe, check and ball valves, CL150 and CL300, flanged, up to 12".

Forged fittings

Forged C.S threaded fittings, CL1000 to CL6000, and forged C.S socket-weld fittings, CL3000 to CL9000, ½" to 4". S.S threaded fittings in 1000, 2000 and 3000 psi from ⅛" to 4", including threadolets. M.S swage nipples in 1000# and 3000# NPT, and long nipples in SCH40 to SCH160.

Instrumentation and gauges

S.S compression fittings with nut and ferrule for 6 to 12 mm tube, S.S needle valves from 1000# to 10000# NPT, and liquid-filled S.S pressure gauges with ranges up to 15,000 PSI.

Flanges and gaskets

MS A105 and SS A182 F304/304L and F316/316L weld neck (WNRF), slip-on, socket weld, blind and threaded flanges, CL150 to CL600, ½" to 24". RTJ ring gaskets in stainless or soft iron, and spiral wound gaskets with graphite or PTFE filler for CL150 to CL1500.

What to send for an oilfield quote

  • Item and size.
  • Class, pressure or figure number.
  • End connection and thread (NPT, BSPT, SW, B/W or flanged).
  • Material grade.
  • Quantity and delivery location.
