About this product

A spring check valve has a spring-loaded disc that closes as soon as flow stops, which limits backflow and water hammer.

ASTM A351 covers cast stainless steel: CF8 is the cast form of 304 and CF8M the cast form of 316. Grade 316 has added molybdenum, which resists chlorides such as seawater and salty air better than 304; 304 is the general-purpose grade. WOG is the rated pressure in psi for water, oil and gas. BSP (British) and NPT (American) threads look alike but do not seal together, so tell us which thread your line uses.
