About this product

A globe valve closes by pressing a disc onto a seat, so it can regulate (throttle) flow as well as shut it off. It causes more pressure drop than a gate valve.

ASTM A182 covers forged stainless steel, in grades such as F304 and F316. Grade 316 has added molybdenum, which resists chlorides such as seawater and salty air better than 304; 304 is the general-purpose grade. WOG is the rated pressure in psi for water, oil and gas. Class 800 is a pressure class for small forged steel valves. SW means socket-weld ends.
