About this product

A short radius elbow bends over 1 times the pipe size, which saves space where room is tight.

Grade 316 has added molybdenum, which resists chlorides such as seawater and salty air better than 304; 304 is the general-purpose grade. The schedule (SCH) sets the wall thickness: the higher the number, the thicker the wall.
