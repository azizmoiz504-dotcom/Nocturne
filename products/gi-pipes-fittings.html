How to choose GI pipe and fittings

G.I pipe is listed in SCH40, class B and class C from ½" to 6". G.I fittings come in CL150 and CL300 with BSP or NPT threads over the same range: 45° and 90° elbows, street elbows, equal and reducing tees, sockets, unions, equal crosses, end caps, plugs, reducing bushes, hex nipples and king nipples. Reducing sockets run from ⅜" × ¼" to 6" × 4". For hose, we also list G.I king nipples in NPT or BSPT from ½" to 12" and G.I hose menders from ½" to 8".

The fittings are galvanised malleable iron to ASME B16.3, with plugs and bushes to B16.14. CL150 suits general water, air and low pressure service; CL300 is the heavier pattern for higher pressure. Check whether the existing pipework is BSP or NPT before you order, because the two do not seal reliably together, and fit a union wherever the line has to come apart.

We also offer pipe threading at the counter. Send the size, class, thread and quantity on WhatsApp and we will quote.

Questions buyers ask

Do you sell GI pipe?

Yes. G.I pipe is listed in SCH40, class B and class C from ½" to 6". Send the size, class and quantity on WhatsApp for a quote.

BSP or NPT GI fittings?

Match the thread on your existing pipe and fittings. We list both, and they should not be mixed.

Class 150 or class 300?

Class 150 for general water and air lines; class 300 where the line runs at higher pressure.

Do you have hose menders and king nipples?

Yes. G.I hose menders are listed from ½" to 8", and G.I king nipples in NPT or BSPT from ½" to 12".

Can you thread GI pipe?

Yes, we offer pipe threading at our Al Quoz counter. Tell us the thread type (BSP or NPT) when you send your list.
