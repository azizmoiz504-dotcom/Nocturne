How to choose GI pipe and fittings

G.I pipe is listed in SCH40, SCH80, class B and class C from ½" to 6". G.I fittings run from ¼" to 6": 45° and 90° elbows, street elbows, equal tees, sockets, unions, crosses, end caps, plugs, hex nipples and king nipples, plus reducing elbows, reducing tees, reducing sockets, reducing bushes and reducing hex nipples from ⅜" × ¼" to 6" × 4". We also list G.I long nipples from ¼" to 6" in lengths of 3" to 12", and G.I hose menders from ½" to 6".

The galvanised finish protects the fittings from rust on water and air lines. Check whether the existing pipework is BSP or NPT before you order, because the two do not seal reliably together, and fit a union wherever the line has to come apart.

Questions buyers ask

Do you sell GI pipe?

Yes. G.I pipe is listed in SCH40, SCH80, class B and class C from ½" to 6". Send the size, schedule or class and quantity on WhatsApp for a quote.

BSP or NPT GI fittings?

Match the thread on your existing pipe and fittings, and tell us which one you need when you order. BSP and NPT should not be mixed.

Class 150 or class 300?

Class 150 for general water and air lines; class 300 where the line runs at higher pressure.

Do you have hose menders and king nipples?

Yes. G.I hose menders are listed from ½" to 6", and G.I king nipples from ¼" to 6".

Can you thread GI pipe?

Yes, we offer pipe threading at our Al Quoz counter. Tell us the thread type (BSP or NPT) when you send your list.
