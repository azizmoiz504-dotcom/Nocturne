How to choose stainless steel pipe and fittings

S.S forged threaded fittings in SS304 and SS316 (ASTM A182) are listed in 2000, 3000 and 6000 psi from ¼" to 4": sockets, half couplings, 45° and 90° elbows, equal and reducing tees, crosses, hex nipples, unions, end caps, end plugs and threadolets. S.S king nipples are listed in 1000, 2000 and 3000 psi from ¼" to 4".

S.S pipe, ERW and seamless, is stocked at Al Quoz in SCH10, SCH20, SCH40, SCH80 and SCH160 from ½" to 12". S.S butt-weld fittings to ASME B16.9 in ASTM A403 304/304L and 316/316L run from ½" to 24" in SCH10, SCH40 and SCH80, and S.S flanges in ASTM A182 F304/304L and F316/316L come in SORF, SWRF, WNRF, blind and threaded up to 24".

316L resists chlorides better than 304, so it is the usual pick near the sea, in cooling water and in chemical lines. Use threaded fittings where the line must come apart, and butt-weld fittings on larger welded runs.

Questions buyers ask

SS 316L or 304?

Our S.S flanges and butt-weld fittings are listed in both 304/304L and 316/316L, and our S.S threaded fittings in SS304 and SS316. For pipe, send the grade your job specifies with your list and we will confirm what we can supply.

What pressure are your SS threaded fittings?

Forged threaded fittings are listed in 2000, 3000 and 6000 psi, and king nipples in 1000, 2000 and 3000 psi, all from ¼" to 4". Send the rating, size and thread (BSP or NPT) with your list.

Do you have SS pipe?

Yes. S.S pipe, ERW and seamless, is listed in SCH10 to SCH160 from ½" to 12".

What is a threadolet?

A branch fitting welded onto a run pipe to give a threaded outlet; a weldolet gives a butt-weld outlet instead. We list S.S threadolets from ¼" to 4".
