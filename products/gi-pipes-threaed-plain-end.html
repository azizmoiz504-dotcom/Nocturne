GI Pipes (Threaded & Plain End)

Our GI Pipes (Threaded & Plain End) are available in sizes from ½” to 12” with various wall thicknesses and standard lengths. We supply high-quality galvanized steel pipes with threaded ends, plain ends, and matching fittings for water supply, firefighting, fencing, HVAC, drainage, and general construction applications. Corrosion-resistant finish, durable performance, and ready stock available.
