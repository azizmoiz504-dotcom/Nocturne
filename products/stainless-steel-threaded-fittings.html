How to choose stainless steel threaded fittings

S.S forged threaded fittings in SS304 and SS316 (ASTM A182) are listed in 2000, 3000 and 6000 psi, BSP or NPT, from ¼" to 2": sockets, half couplings, 45° and 90° elbows, equal tees, crosses, hex nipples, unions, end caps, end plugs and threadolets, plus reducing sockets, reducing tees and reducing hex nipples from ⅜" × ¼" to 2" × 1½". S.S king nipples are listed from ¼" to 4", and S.S long nipples from ¼" to 8" in lengths of 3" to 12".

Need the pipe as well? S.S pipe, ERW and seamless, in SCH10, SCH40 and SCH80 from ¼" to 12", and S.S butt-weld fittings up to 12", are on our stainless steel pipes and butt-weld fittings page. S.S flanges in ASTM A182 F304/304L and F316/316L come in SORF, SWRF, WNRF, blind and threaded up to 24".

316L resists chlorides better than 304, so it is the usual pick near the sea, in cooling water and in chemical lines. Use threaded fittings where the line must come apart, and butt-weld fittings on larger welded runs.

Questions buyers ask

SS 316L or 304?

Our S.S flanges and butt-weld fittings are listed in both 304/304L and 316/316L, and our S.S threaded fittings in SS304 and SS316. For pipe, send the grade your job specifies with your list and we will confirm what we can supply.

What pressure are your SS threaded fittings?

Forged threaded fittings are listed in 2000, 3000 and 6000 psi from ¼" to 2". Send the rating, size and thread (BSP or NPT) with your list.

Do you have SS pipe?

Yes. S.S pipe, ERW and seamless, is listed in SCH10, SCH40 and SCH80 from ¼" to 12".

What is a threadolet?

A branch fitting welded onto a run pipe to give a threaded outlet. We list S.S threadolets in 2000, 3000 and 6000 psi, BSP or NPT, from ¼" to 2".
