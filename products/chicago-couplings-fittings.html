How to choose Chicago couplings

We list G.I Chicago couplings in Dubai for air and water hose in two size groups. The 2-lug range, ¼" to 1", has hose end couplings and female and male couplings with BSPT thread, plus a 1" 3-way connector. The 4-lug range, 1¼" to 2", has hose ends and female and male ends with BSP thread. G.I king nipples and hose menders are on our G.I fittings page, and four bolt clamps on our hose clamp page.

Chicago couplings, also called claw couplings, join with a quarter turn, and two claw heads connect with no male or female half. Match the hose end to the inside diameter of the hose. Rubber washers for Chicago couplings are on our gaskets page.

For a quote, WhatsApp the part, size, 2-lug or 4-lug, thread where it applies, and the quantity.

Questions buyers ask

2-lug or 4-lug?

The 2-lug couplings cover ¼" to 1" and the 4-lug couplings cover 1¼" to 2". Pick by the size of your hose.

Which size of hose end do I need?

Match the hose end to the inside diameter of your hose. We list ¼" to 1" and 1¼" to 2" groups.

Where are the king nipples and hose menders?

On our G.I fittings page: G.I king nipples in NPT and BSPT from ½" to 12", and G.I hose menders from ½" to 8".
