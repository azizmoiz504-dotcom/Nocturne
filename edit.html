How to use the editor

Drag a card to move it (first = top of the list). Drop a photo from your computer on a picture, press Choose photo, or click a card and press Ctrl+V to paste a copied photo. Copy makes a second card with the same details. Move to another section sends a card to another category. Type straight into the boxes to change names, specs, sizes or the category name. Material decides which Material filter the product shows under on the website; pick "Other" to type your own (for more than one, separate them with commas, e.g. "Brass, Stainless").

The category's main photo (the round one at the top) works the same way: drop a photo on it, click it and press Ctrl+V, press Choose photo, or press "Make main photo" on a card. When finished, press Upload to website. It tidies and checks your changes, shows you anything to look at, puts them on the website and tells you when they are live (usually a few minutes). Your work is kept in this browser if you close the page. Save changes file is a backup: it saves your changes in Downloads so you can send them to Claude instead.
