Fakhri Tools · Product Editor

Drag a card to move it (first = top of the list). Drop a photo from your computer on a card's picture, or click a card and press Ctrl+V to paste a copied photo. Copy makes a second card with the same details. Type straight into the boxes to change names, specs or sizes. When finished, press Save changes file, keep it in Downloads and tell Claude "done". Your work is kept in this browser if you close the page.
