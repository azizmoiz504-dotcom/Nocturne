UAE delivery

Pipe fittings delivery across the UAE from Dubai

We deliver pipes, fittings, flanges, valves and hoses anywhere in the UAE for orders from AED 5,000. Smaller orders can be collected from our counter in Al Quoz, Dubai.

Where we deliver

Anywhere in the UAE. That includes Abu Dhabi, Musaffah and Jebel Ali, as well as sites and workshops across Dubai.

The minimum order for delivery is AED 5,000. Below that, you are welcome to collect from our trade counter in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3.

How a delivery order works

Everything starts with your list on WhatsApp. We do not take orders through the website.

  • Send your list on WhatsApp with sizes, classes, end connections, materials and quantities.
  • We confirm what we can supply and send you a quote.
  • You confirm the order and the delivery address, and we agree the delivery details with you.
  • Pay by cash, card, bank transfer or cheque.

What to send for a delivery quote

  • Each item: size, class or pressure (for example CL150, PN16, 3000#, SCH40), end connection (flanged, NPT, BSPT, SW or B/W) and material (MS, CS, SS 316L, GI, brass).
  • Quantity for each line.
  • Delivery location: emirate, area and site or company name.
  • A contact name and number on site.

What we deliver

Valves, CS, SS and GI fittings, flanges, gaskets, camlocks and couplings, hoses, gauges and oilfield items such as hammer unions. Browse the categories below and send us the list.
