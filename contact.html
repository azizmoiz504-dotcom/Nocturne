How to find us

Al Quoz lies between Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Khail Road, and both roads give access to the industrial areas. Head for Al Quoz Industrial Area 3 and find 8th Street.

Ajyad Traditional Kitchen is at the start of our row; use it as your landmark and find us at Warehouse 8. If you cannot find us, call or WhatsApp and we will guide you in.

If you work in Al Quoz Industrial Area 1, 2 or 4, we are close by in Area 3. Call +971 4 285 0135 if you need directions on the way.

What to bring or send

A clear list saves time at the counter. Send it on WhatsApp before you leave, or bring it with you.

  • Size of each item, in inches or mm.
  • Class or pressure: CL150, PN16, 800 WOG, 3000#, SCH40 and so on.
  • End connection: flanged, NPT, BSPT, SW or B/W.
  • Material: MS, CS, SS 316L, GI, brass or bronze.
  • Quantity of each item.
  • For a replacement part, bring the old one or send a clear photo with a tape measure beside it.
  • For hose crimping, bring the old hose if you have it.
