Flange chart: Class 150 and Class 300 dimensions, and PN16 vs JIS 10K

Outside diameter, bolt circle (PCD), number of bolts and bolt size for ASME B16.5 Class 150 and Class 300 flanges from ½" to 24", with a plain guide to why Class 150, PN16 and JIS 10K flanges are not the same.

How to read the chart

NPS is the nominal pipe size. The flange OD is the outside diameter of the flange plate. The bolt circle, or PCD (pitch circle diameter), is the circle through the centres of the bolt holes. Two flanges bolt together only when the PCD, the number of bolts and the bolt size all match.

For the same pipe size, a Class 300 flange is larger than a Class 150 flange and usually has more or bigger bolts, so the two classes do not bolt together. Weld neck, slip-on, socket weld, threaded and blind flanges of the same class and size share the same OD and drilling.

Class 150 vs PN16 vs JIS 10K

These are three different flange systems. Class 150 comes from the American standard ASME B16.5 and is drilled in inch dimensions. PN16 comes from the European standard EN 1092-1 and is drilled in metric dimensions with metric bolts. JIS 10K comes from the Japanese standard JIS B2220 and has its own metric drilling.

Although the pressure ratings sound similar, the OD, the bolt circle, the bolt count and the bolt size differ at most sizes. In general a Class 150 flange will not bolt to a PN16 or a JIS 10K flange, and a PN16 flange will not bolt to a JIS 10K flange. Even where a bolt circle comes close, the holes or the bolts may not line up properly, so do not force a mixed joint.

Valves, pumps and equipment from different countries often arrive with different flanges. Before you order, check the marking on the mating flange or the drawing, or measure the OD, the PCD and the bolt holes and send them to us.

  • PN10, PN16 or PN25: EN 1092-1, metric drilling.
  • 5K, 10K or 16K: JIS B2220, metric drilling.

Questions buyers ask

Will a Class 150 flange bolt to a PN16 flange?

Not in general. The bolt circle, the number of bolts and the bolt size differ at most sizes. Check the standard on the mating flange and order the same one.

How do I identify a flange I already have?

Read the marking stamped on the rim, which usually shows the size, the class, PN or K, and the material. If there is no marking, measure the OD, the bolt circle (PCD), the number of holes and the hole size, and send them on WhatsApp.

Do Class 150 and Class 300 flanges share the same drilling?

No. A Class 300 flange of the same size has a larger OD and bolt circle, and often more or bigger bolts.
