Pipe schedule chart: SCH 40, 80, 160 and XXS in mm

Outside diameter and wall thickness for steel pipe from ½" to 24" (22" not listed), with weight per metre for SCH 40 and SCH 80 carbon steel pipe.

How to read the chart

The nominal pipe size (NPS) is a name, not a measurement. For every NPS the outside diameter stays the same, and the schedule sets the wall thickness. A higher schedule means a thicker wall and a smaller bore.

From 8" upwards, XXS is thinner than SCH 160, so do not assume XXS is always the heaviest wall.

How the weight is worked out

Carbon steel weight in kg/m = (OD − wall) × wall × 0.02466, with OD and wall in mm, rounded to 2 decimals.

These values are nominal, per ASME B36.10M. Wall thickness has a mill tolerance, so confirm the actual dimensions against the mill certificate before you design or weld to them.

Questions buyers ask

Is SCH 40 the same as STD?

Up to 10" they share the same wall thickness. Above that they differ, so always state the schedule you need.

Why is XXS thinner than SCH 160 at 8" and above?

XXS and the numbered schedules come from different series. From 8" upwards SCH 160 has the thicker wall.
