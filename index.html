01

Marine

Shipyards, vessel operators and ship chandlers depend on bronze valves, Storz couplings and gaskets that hold up against salt, vibration and long voyages.

  • Bronze gate & globe
  • Storz couplings
  • Safety valves
  • Spiral wound gaskets
02

Offshore

Rigs and offshore support vessels need correct pressure ratings and fast turnarounds. We keep forged fittings, A182 flanges and hammer unions on the shelf.

  • Forged steel fittings
  • A182 316L flanges
  • Hammer unions
  • RTJ gaskets
03

Oilfield

From wellhead to tank farm: high-pressure ball valves, Chicago couplings, king nipples and hammer unions built for mud, sand and pressure.

  • C.S ball valves 3000 WOG
  • Chicago couplings
  • Hammer unions
  • Swage nipples
04

Energy

Power and utilities projects need large-bore butterfly and gate valves, expansion connectors and pipe up to 24 inches.

  • Butterfly valves to 24"
  • Flexible flange connectors
  • Universal couplings
  • Pressure gauges
05

Industrial

Factories, MEP contractors and plant maintenance teams buy everything from ¼" instrumentation fittings to Y-strainers and camlocks — from one counter.

  • Y-strainers
  • Camlock couplings
  • Needle valves
  • QRC Ludecke
