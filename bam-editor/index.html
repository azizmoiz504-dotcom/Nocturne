Product Editor

How to use the editor

Pick a category at the top, or search for any product or part number. Type straight into the boxes to change a name, a size, a part number or a price. Leave a price empty if it is on request.

+ Add size adds a line to a product, and ✕ removes a line. + Add product adds a new card to a group, Copy makes a second card with the same details, and Delete removes a card. Move a card with the ↑ ↓ buttons, by dragging it on a computer, or to another group with Move to.

New category makes a whole new product range (like Hoses or Safety Items) with its first group; then add its products with + Add product, and give it a picture at the top. New group adds a group inside the category you are in.

To change a photo, tap it and choose a photo, drop a photo on it, or click the card and press Ctrl+V. The category picture at the top works the same way.

Your work is kept on this phone or computer if you close the page. When you finish, press Save changes file and send the file you get. New cards show a green edge and changed cards an orange edge.
